BEFORE YOU USE IT

The details, when you need them.

v0.15.0 media & testing boundaries

Bundled images are AI-generated placeholders, not real people, client work or real brand logos. Replace them with your own media. Six bundled logos are fictional marks.

Panorama dragging was tested with synthetic pointer events and keyboard, not real touch hardware.

Video sources load only when a visitor opens a configured card. YouTube/Vimeo URLs were checked structurally, not opened on the real networks. The portfolio demo ships static covers with no playable video source.

Audio Player ships no audio files. Its cover is bundled imagery and playback uses native audio with preload="none".

The local empty-settings renders produced one Elementor core PHP warning about an undefined _id key. The report attributes zero warnings to the plugin. CDN/offload media plugins were not tested.

Setup & content requirements

Booking/Appointments, Countdown Timer and WhatsApp Chat Button render nothing on the live page until configured. Default-state editor previews and built-in visuals do not mean these widgets are ready to use without setup. Table of Contents needs page headings, and Form Styler needs a form before they render.

Live Search uses only this site's REST API. The category filter needs JavaScript; without JavaScript, visitors get a normal search form. Results are rendered as text, not HTML.

Form Styler does not store submissions. Mailto mode opens the visitor's own email app; URL mode needs an HTTPS endpoint you control. The hidden trap field targets simple spam bots, not every kind of spam.

Data Table accepts plain-text rows and cells, sorts in the browser and stacks rows into labeled cards on phones. Calculator needs JavaScript and is hidden without it. Filterable Portfolio keeps all projects visible without JavaScript.

Countdown Timer uses one fixed end moment in the site timezone, shared by every visitor. Live digits need JavaScript; without it, the end date is shown as text.

Table of Contents is generated from page headings in the browser and is hidden without JavaScript. Choose its scroll offset to fit fixed headers. Its reported overflow checks do not certify that other page content cannot overflow.

Content Switcher supports sanitized basic HTML, not scripts or arbitrary embeds. Logo Marquee needs your own logo media; no licensed logo collection is bundled.

Social, reviews & media

Instagram Feed is a manual photo grid using your own local media-library images. It never contacts Instagram, has no login/API/embed integration and does not fetch new posts automatically. Optional post/follow links only open on click. Photo rights, live social links and CDN-offloaded media were not verified.

Social Share uses plain links, not an SDK, tracking or share counters. Network URL formats were checked for structure only, not opened on the real services. Copy-link needs HTTPS or localhost; device sharing depends on browser support and was tested with a stub.

Star Rating & Reviews displays owner-entered ratings and text; it does not collect or verify reviews. Reviews need a valid rating from 1 to 5. No review markup for search engines is generated.

WhatsApp Chat Button is a plain outbound WhatsApp link, not a live-chat backend or tracking script. Announcement Bar dismissal can be remembered for a configured duration or tab session; change the announcement ID when the message should return.

Lottie uses your own same-site JSON files and the bundled light player's feature set, not every Lottie feature. WordPress may require a separately configured JSON upload policy or plugin. No remote animation service is used; the v0.15.0 default-render audit used bundled visuals with no uploaded media.

Reels Gallery uses your own MP4 or WebM files from the media library. It is not an Instagram or YouTube embed, and it never autoplays with sound.

Motion, layout & browser behavior

Sticky Scroll Column uses native CSS sticky positioning and never sticks on phones. Where sticky is unsupported, the panel simply stacks. Horizontal Scroll Section is a native horizontal strip rather than a page-scroll hijack; without JavaScript, its optional controls disappear but native scrolling remains.

Scroll Snap Sections snap inside their own scroll box, not across the whole page. Their dot links are plain anchors, and smooth scrolling is disabled with reduced motion.

Mouse Cursor Effects requires a hover-capable fine pointer and JavaScript. It never hides the system cursor, is disabled with reduced motion and on touch screens, and activates only one instance per page. Touch and assistive-technology behavior was not tested.

SVG Text Path follows the browser's textLength spacingAndGlyphs behavior. Arabic-script shaping and stretching can differ between browsers and were not tested. It uses fixed paths rather than user-supplied SVG; optional rotation is disabled with reduced motion.

Use one Exit-Intent Popup per page. Desktop users can trigger it by moving the mouse to the top edge after its arming time, or by delay. Touch devices only receive the delay trigger; the editor shows a preview instead of opening the popup.

Dark Mode Toggle provides a page class, background/text styling and variables, not automatic recoloring of every theme or widget. Theme-specific CSS may be needed for hard-coded colors or third-party components. Use one toggle.

Reading Progress Bar measures the whole document, not an individual article or reading time. Fixed bars or percentages can overlap theme headers and other fixed controls. Choose top/bottom placement and percentage settings on staging, and use one bar per page.

Booking requests & WooCommerce

Booking/Appointments composes an appointment REQUEST in the visitor's mail app. It does not check availability, hold a time, confirm a booking, store an entry, send server email, sync a calendar, take payment or send reminders. The visitor must send the email and wait for confirmation. Mail-app delivery was not tested; native no-JavaScript mode cannot enforce the custom weekday/time policy.

Product Grid, Add to Cart and Quick View require WooCommerce, installed separately. WooCommerce handles pricing, tax, stock rules, sessions and checkout. Variable, grouped, external or non-purchasable items lead to the local product page. Variation selection, grouped/external purchases, checkout, payment gateways, shipping, refunds, coupons, multicurrency and inventory races were not tested or newly implemented.

Privacy, access & caching

Cookie Consent Notice records an Accept/Reject choice in localStorage and emits a browser event. It does not block, delete or scan cookies or scripts, and is not legal compliance. It requires JavaScript and separate integration with any analytics or ad scripts.

Protected Content gates sanitized text from a private standard WordPress post through a logged-in, nonce-checked request. It requires JavaScript, a saved public page and an author allowed to read the source. Allowed roles are explicit; administrators are not automatically allowed, and an empty role list denies everyone. It does not execute media, shortcodes, embeds or Elementor templates.

For Protected Content, exclude signed-in pages and admin-ajax.php from caches; an expired nonce fails closed and needs a reload. This is not DRM or file/media protection, and it does not secure other plugins, public media URLs, backups or compromised accounts. Authorized readers can save the text. Site editors can change its policy.

Conditional Block is a display rule, not access control or security. Do not put confidential content or private links in it. Exclude pages using visitor-specific conditions from page caching, because a cache can serve one visitor's result to another.

Maintenance mode boundaries

Coming Soon / Maintenance is a page block only. It does not disable the site, send HTTP 503, add noindex or stop search indexing. The portfolio review's own noindex settings are separate and remain enabled.
