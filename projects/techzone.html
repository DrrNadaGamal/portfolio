CREDIT

Product direction, scope and delivery: Nada Gamal. Presented as a demonstration build, not a production client deployment.

The problem

An electronics shopper compares many similar products: they need to filter by category, sort by price, check specifications and see delivery and warranty terms before they trust a store.

What was built

A static store in Arabic (right to left) with an English version under /en and a language switch in the navigation, 102 pages in all. It has a catalog with live search, a category filter and five sort orders, six category pages, 36 product pages with a specifications table and related products, a deals page with a flash-sale countdown and stock bars, a brands page, about, an FAQ with ten questions and a contact form with validation. The cart works in the browser: quantities, totals and free shipping over SAR 200. The device artwork is original SVG, the font is self-hosted and there are no external requests.

Why it matters

A shopper can narrow a large catalog quickly, compare specifications and see delivery terms before adding to the cart. This is a design for that flow, not a measured result from a real store.

Limits

Products, prices and ratings are sample data. The cart and the contact form keep data only in the visitor's browser, and there are no real payments.
