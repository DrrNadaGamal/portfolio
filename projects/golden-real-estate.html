CREDIT

Product direction, scope and delivery: Nada Gamal. Presented as a demonstration build, not a production client deployment.

The problem

A property buyer decides from pictures, price and location in the first few seconds, and a listing page that is slow to scan or missing details sends them to a competitor.

What was built

A static website in Arabic (right to left) with a matching English version under /en, 13 Arabic pages and 17 English pages. It has a hero slider, a property list with filter and sort, six property pages with a gallery, price and agent card, services, team, a blog with three full articles in each language, and a contact form with validation. Every page links to its counterpart in the other language, and the images are local.

Why it matters

A visitor can scan listings, open a full property page and switch language without losing their place, and the agency gets an inquiry that names the property. This is a design for that flow, not a measured result from a real agency.

Limits

Properties, prices in SAR, agents and articles are sample data and the footer says so. The contact form stores nothing outside the visitor's browser.
