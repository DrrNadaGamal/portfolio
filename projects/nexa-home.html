Nexa Home

A smart home store with a catalog, install packages and a working demo cart. Arabic and English.

Nexa Home · Home page. Sample content.

CREDIT

Product direction, scope and delivery: Nada Gamal. Presented as a demonstration build, not a production client deployment.

The problem

Smart home devices are hard to buy one by one: a shopper does not know what works together, what installation costs, or which pieces to start with.

What was built

A static store in Arabic (right to left) and English with 27 pages per language: a home page with a smart-hub panel, a product list with search, sort and category filter, five category pages, 14 product pages with specifications and related items, a features page, three install packages, about, FAQ and a contact form with validation. The cart works in the browser: quantities, a count badge and a total. The device artwork is original SVG, the font is self-hosted and there are no external requests.

Why it matters

A shopper can start from a package or a single device, see the specifications beside the price and add items to a cart without leaving the page. This is a design for that flow, not a measured result from a real store.

Limits

Products, prices and reviews are sample data and the footer says so. The cart and the contact form keep data only in the visitor's browser, and there are no real payments.
